What to do now

  1. Open a new chat in the AI you are moving to.
  2. Paste the pack (part 1 first, if there are several). Wait for each part to be acknowledged.
  3. Your AI replies with two documents: KNOWLEDGE_BLUEPRINT.md and a standing instructions file. Save both.
  4. Put the instructions file where your AI reads standing instructions (a Project's instructions, or the file named in it). Keep the blueprint beside your documents.
