Your documents

When you choose a folder, HANDOVER reads it inside your browser tab. The files, the map and the pack it builds are never sent to us or to anyone else. When you close the tab, they are gone.

You decide what goes into the pack, and you paste it into your own AI yourself. From that point the terms of your AI provider apply, not ours. Check your AI account's data settings before you paste anything sensitive.

Stored in your browser

  • Your licence key is saved in your browser's local storage so you do not have to re-enter it. Clear your browser data to remove it.
  • Your payment reference is kept in session storage on the confirmation page only, so a refresh can finish confirming your payment. It is cleared when you close the tab.

The checkout page

The checkout page (/checkout/) loads Stripe's payment form. Stripe uses cookies and similar technologies there, to process the payment and prevent fraud. For that processing Stripe acts under its own privacy policy, at stripe.com/privacy. No other page on this site loads anything from Stripe or any other company.

Who else is involved

  • Stripe: payments. Purchases may be sold through Onelink (Stripe's Link) as merchant of record, which then handles tax, receipts and payment support under its own privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare: the licence service.
  • GitHub: website hosting.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, the transfer relies on safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK–US data bridge or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.
