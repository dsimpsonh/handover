Why switch

Your AI provider shouldn't own your know-how.

Prices rise

A plan goes up per seat, or the features you rely on move to a higher tier. Moving should cost an afternoon, not two years of setup.

Limits and outages

Usage caps and downtime hit when you're busiest. A second provider ready to go is cheap insurance.

A better model launches

The best model for your work changes every few months. Try it with everything your business knows, not a blank chat.

Your team standardises

A client, a partner or your own team settles on another tool. Your rules and decisions go with you.

HANDOVER turns what's spread across one AI's projects and your folders into a Knowledge Blueprint any AI can read. Run it again before every switch, or keep it current so you're never locked in.

Example

Two years on Claude. Moving on.

A ten-person design studio has used Claude every day for two years. Pricing is going up for their plan, and they want to try another model for client work. Here's what they had, and what HANDOVER gives them.

What they had
  • 14 Claude Projects, each with its own instructions and uploaded files
  • 9 skills they wrote themselves: quote writer, client onboarding, brand voice…
  • Canonical documents: pricing v7, the studio handbook, contract templates, the decisions log
  • 2,340 files across Drive folders, with old versions next to current ones
  • No blueprint. The rules lived in people's heads, prompts and one AI's setup
What HANDOVER does
  1. They paste each project's instructions into a document, gather the project files and their skills folder, and point HANDOVER at those plus their Drive folders.
  2. It maps all 2,340 files in the browser: what links to what, which skills and instructions use which documents, and the rules and decisions written in them, each with file and line.
  3. They untick HR and client folders and keep emails and phone numbers blanked.
  4. They pick the AI they're moving to. HANDOVER builds a pack sized to fit it.
What they get
  • A Knowledge Blueprint: how they price, the rules they never break, the decisions and why, each pointing to its source document
  • A standing instructions file for the new AI, built from their projects and skills
  • Day-one answers in their own terms, from the first chat
  • A blueprint they own, ready for the next switch

Tip: chat history isn't read yet. Save the conversations that hold important decisions as documents in a folder, and HANDOVER reads them like any other file.

How it works

Four steps, about ten minutes.

Choose your folders

Point HANDOVER at the folders your work lives in. It reads Word, PDF, web pages and text files right here in your browser.

See the map

It finds every file, how they link, and the rules and decisions written in them, each with the file and line it came from.

Choose what your AI sees

Untick anything private, leave out folders, blank out names. Email addresses and phone numbers are blanked by default.

Paste one pack

Paste the pack into the AI you are moving to. It writes your Knowledge Blueprint and a standing instructions file, ready to use.

Privacy

Your files never leave your computer.

HANDOVER reads your folders inside this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded to us. You decide which excerpts go into the pack, and you paste it into your own AI yourself.

Once you paste the pack, your AI provider's own terms apply. Check your account's data settings before you paste anything sensitive. Read the privacy notice.
